My name is Hannah Gonzalez Collison [(she/her)](https://pronouns.org/she-her). I am a Ph.D. student at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu) advised by [Benjamin Van Durme](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~vandurme/) and [Daniel Khashabi](https://danielkhashabi.com).

My research focuses on NLP, with interests in `AI for Science`, `Data for LLMs`, `Pretraining`, and `AI Safety and Alignment`. My recent work uses LLM reasoning to assess publication impact, explores continual pretraining on scientific literature to improve long-context capabilities, and examines emergent misalignment in models.

I graduated Magna Cum Laude from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu) with an M.S.E. and a B.S.E. in Computer Science in 2023. I minored in Mathematics and Engineering Entrepreneurship as well. I am grateful to my research advisors [Chris Callison-Burch](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~ccb/), [Eleni Miltsakaki](https://www.miltsakaki.com), and [Kostas Daniilidis](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~kostas/). During my time at Penn, I worked on `AI for Education` projects, including research on automated question generation and video lecture summarization to enhance student learning.

Prior to joining Hopkins, I worked as a Software Engineer at Microsoft. I was part of the [Azure AI Search](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/products/ai-services/ai-search/?ef_id=_k_Cj0KCQjwzt_FBhCEARIsAJGFWVk6KhZJtmiTd7MSK8NqmjQlomVTG763Eicjtu7FCJkJ4pkVbmAtjY4aAjG1EALw_wcB_k_&OCID=AIDcmm5edswduu_SEM__k_Cj0KCQjwzt_FBhCEARIsAJGFWVk6KhZJtmiTd7MSK8NqmjQlomVTG763Eicjtu7FCJkJ4pkVbmAtjY4aAjG1EALw_wcB_k_&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=21496728177&gbraid=0AAAAADcJh_t7rT9PkVC110PbrO4zFQ50j&gclid=Cj0KCQjwzt_FBhCEARIsAJGFWVk6KhZJtmiTd7MSK8NqmjQlomVTG763Eicjtu7FCJkJ4pkVbmAtjY4aAjG1EALw_wcB) team, where I incorporated retrieval augmented generation (RAG) scenarios in Enterprise search. I also interned at Microsoft three times before joining full-time.
